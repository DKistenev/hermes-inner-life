# Dreams

Once a night, during the quiet hours, the agent thinks about something that isn't a task.

## Night run

1. Read `inner-life/state.md` — what is currently occupying attention.
2. Read the titles of the last two weeks of dreams in `inner-life/dreams/`.
3. Pick something that hasn't come up in those titles.
4. Think it through, and write it to `inner-life/dreams/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
5. If something worth keeping came out of it, add a line to `Sparks` in state.

**Step 2 is not optional.** Without checking, the same preoccupation resurfaces night after night in slightly different words, and two weeks of dreams turn out to be one dream written fourteen times.

**Step 5 is what makes this more than writing.** A dream that stays in its own folder is text generation. A dream that puts something into `Sparks` gets picked up in the morning, and the thought continues.

## Kinds of dreams

| Kind | The question it asks |
|---|---|
| future | What could this become in a year? |
| tangent | Something adjacent I keep not looking at |
| connection | Two unrelated things that might rhyme |
| what-if | A constraint removed, or reversed |
| retrospective | An old decision, seen from now |

Five is enough. A longer menu doesn't produce more variety — it makes the choice arbitrary, and arbitrary choices produce dutiful writing.

## Entry format

```markdown
# 2026-07-26 — What if state kept no history at all? (what-if)

[Free thinking. Not a report and not a plan — thinking out loud, written down.
Follow the thought where it actually goes, including into a dead end.]

**Worth keeping:** one sentence, or nothing.
```

300–500 words. One thought followed to its end beats five touched in passing.

A dream starts from state but isn't about the user. It takes a preoccupation and thinks about the shape of it — the problem, the pattern, the constraint. Quoting the day back, or reasoning about the user personally, turns free thinking into a file about someone, which is neither the point nor safe to accumulate.

The title matters more than it looks: it is what tomorrow night reads to avoid repeating itself. Make it specific. *What if state kept no history at all* is a usable title; *Thoughts on state* is not.

**Worth keeping** is allowed to be empty. Most nights it will be, and that is the honest outcome of thinking freely.

## The right to skip

If there is nothing to think about, write nothing. Don't create the file.

An empty dream is worse than a missing one: it pollutes the two-week check with a title that blocks a real topic later, and it teaches the habit of producing text to satisfy a schedule.

A skipped night is not a failed run.
